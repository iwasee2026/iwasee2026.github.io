Keynote Speaker Profile

IWASEE 2026


Prof. Dr. Fatima (Hina) Kakepoto

Zhejiang Normal University, China

ResearchGate Google Scholar
← Back to Keynotes

Research Focus

"Innovation and Emerging Technologies for Future Global Connectivity"

Biography:Prof. Dr. Fatima (Hina) Kakepoto Geraldo dos Santos received the B.Sc. degree in Electrical Engineering from the Pontifical Catholic University of Minas Gerais (PUC Minas), Brazil, in 2006, the M.Sc. degree in Electrical Engineering from the Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG), Brazil, in 2011, and the Ph.D. degree in Electrical Engineering from the Federal University of Bahia (UFBA), Brazil, in 2018. He has more than twenty years of professional experience in Electrical Engineering, Telecommunications, Radio Frequency (RF) Systems, and Electromagnetic Compatibility (EMC). For over eleven years, he worked in EMC laboratories, specializing in compliance testing according to CISPR standards, European Directives, and IEC and ISO regulations. At FIAT Automóveis, he coordinated EMC activities for several vehicle development programs, including the Punto, Bravo, Idea, Linea, and Palio Electric projects. He actively participated in the Brazilian Committee of Electricity, Electronics, Lighting, and Telecommunications (ABNT CB-03), served as Secretary of the Brazilian Automotive Electromagnetic Compatibility Committee (ABNT CB-05) from 2008 to 2012, and chaired the Electromagnetic Compatibility Subcommittee (CT-08) of the National Institute of Metrology, Quality and Technology (INMETRO). After successfully passing a public competitive examination, he joined the Institute for Technological Research (IPT/USP) as a Researcher, where he contributed to the accreditation process of the EMC Laboratory in accordance with INMETRO requirements. He also served as Professor and Coordinator of the Electrical Engineering Program at Faculdade Independente do Nordeste (FAINOR), where he taught undergraduate courses in Analog Electronics and Power Electronics. He is currently a Full-Time Professor with the Federal Institute of Education, Science and Technology of Bahia (IFBA), Brazil, where he conducts teaching, research, and innovation activities in Electrical Engineering. He is also affiliated with the Professional Master's Program in Systems and Product Engineering (PPGESP), serving as the Local Coordinator of the PPGESP at the IFBA Vitória da Conquista Campus. In addition, he coordinates EMBRAPII-funded research and innovation projects at the IFBA Innovation Hub. Dr. Santos serves as a reviewer for the IEEE Latin America Transactions (ISSN 1548-0992) and the Journal of Microwaves, Optoelectronics and Electromagnetic Applications (JMOe) (ISSN 1516-7399). He is a Member of IEEE and has served as Faculty Advisor of the IEEE Microwave Theory and Technology Society (MTT-S) Student Branch Chapter at IFBA–Vitória da Conquista since August 2021. His research interests include electromagnetic compatibility, applied electromagnetics, antennas, radio-frequency systems, telecommunications, electromagnetic measurements, electromagnetic sensing, and artificial intelligence applications in engineering.
