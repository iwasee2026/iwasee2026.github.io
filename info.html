General Information

About the IWASEE Workshop

About IWASEE

IWASEE (International Workshop on Advanced Systems Efficiency Engineering) is an international conference on Computer Science and its Applications, bringing together advances in computing with engineering domains such as Green Computing, Artificial Intelligence, Robotics, Radio Frequency technologies (RFID), Internet of Things (IoT), and Wireless Systems to address the challenges of efficient, sustainable, and connected systems.

Modern engineering systems — from energy-aware computing infrastructures to autonomous robots and connected IoT networks — face a defining challenge: as they grow in complexity, ensuring their efficiency, sustainability, and reliability becomes exponentially harder. Advances in artificial intelligence, green computing, and wireless systems are reshaping what is possible, yet researchers across Latin America and North America still have relatively few dedicated venues for deep, interdisciplinary exchange in these converging fields.

Why IWASEE?

IWASEE was born from the conviction that bridging Computer Science with green computing, intelligent automation, robotics, RFID, IoT, and wireless systems engineering is not just academically valuable—it is essential for the next generation of efficient, sustainable, and connected systems.

Initiated by the KR – Knowledge Representation Research Group, part of the Department of Computer Science at Universidad Católica San Pablo (Peru), in collaboration with researchers from Latin America and North America, IWASEE creates a focused, high-quality forum where researchers, practitioners, and industry professionals can present cutting-edge work, engage in rigorous discussion, and forge lasting international collaborations. Supported by the IEEE Region 9 community, particularly the IEEE Microwave Theory and Technology Society (MTT-S), IWASEE also promotes stronger connections between academia, industry, and professional organizations across the Americas.

Mission & Vision

Mission

IWASEE's mission is to provide an international, interdisciplinary forum for advancing Computer Science and its applications through the exchange of scientific and technological innovations in Green Computing, Artificial Intelligence, Robotics and Automation, Radio Frequency Technologies (RFID), Internet of Things (IoT), and Wireless Systems Engineering. We bring together researchers, engineers, and industry professionals to share novel approaches that improve how intelligent, efficient, sustainable, and connected systems are designed, developed, and deployed.

Vision

We envision IWASEE as a leading international conference in Computer Science and its applications, recognized for fostering transformative research that bridges computing with engineering disciplines to advance efficient, sustainable, and intelligent systems. Through collaboration among academia, industry, and professional societies across the Americas, IWASEE aims to build a vibrant research community where Green Computing, Artificial Intelligence, Robotics, RFID, IoT, and Wireless Systems converge to address real-world challenges and shape the next generation of advanced engineering solutions.
